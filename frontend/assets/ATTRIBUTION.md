The source pack is preserved locally for the original neighborhood. The current Spatial Studio uses original procedural buildings, trees, furniture, cars, pedestrians and glasses. Three.js remains under its MIT license in the installed dependency.

## Photographic materials and sky

The current scene uses **Poly Haven** CC0 materials `asphalt_02`, `pavement_03`, `plastered_wall_02` and the `kloppenheim_06_puresky` HDR sky. The color, normal and roughness maps are stored locally at 1K resolution. Exact source URLs and license are recorded in [materials/sources.json](materials/sources.json).

- Source: https://polyhaven.com
