# Neighborhood assets

**City Kit Suburban 2.0**, by **Kenney** (23 April 2025).

- Original source: https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
- License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 Universal.
- Download: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/city-kit-suburban/2c871b7af2-1745479373/kenney_city-kit-suburban_20.zip
- Original license: `kenney/LICENSE.txt`.
- Included: GLB models and their referenced `Textures/colormap.png` palette.

The source pack is preserved locally to make the neighborhood work without a third-party asset CDN. Buildings, foliage, fences and planters use these models. Roads, paving, street signs, benches, lamps, fountain, survey avatar and the test crate are generated by the project's own scene code. Three.js remains under its MIT license in the installed dependency.
